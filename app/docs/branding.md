# Branding
### **Brand Name:** Alink

**Subtitle:** Your Digital Contact, One Tap Away

**Vision:**
To simplify personal and professional introductions by giving everyone a fast, elegant, and shareable digital card accessible via link or QR code. Alink makes networking seamless, memorable, and eco-friendly.

**Description:**
Alink is a minimalist digital visiting card platform that lets you share your name, city, email, and phone through a single link or QR code. No apps, no bulky profiles—just clean, instant connections. Perfect for professionals, creatives, and anyone who wants their contact info always at hand.
